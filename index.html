He Sun (孙赫)

Assistant Professor of Biomedical Engineering & Physics (Boya Scholar)
College of Future Technology, Peking University
Academy for Advanced Interdisciplinary Studies, Peking University
National Biomedical Imaging Center, Peking University

I build frontier imaging instruments that extend human vision for scientific discovery and advanced manufacturing. My lab at Peking University combines physics-grounded algorithms—computational physics, control, and generative AI—with hardware innovations for imaging at extreme scales. My work has supported various challenging science missions, such as black hole imaging and the search for Earth-like exoplanets, as well as biomedical imaging modalities including ultrasound tomography and computational microscopy.
